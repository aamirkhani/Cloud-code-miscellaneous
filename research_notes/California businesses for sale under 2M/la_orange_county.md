# Businesses for Sale in Los Angeles County and Orange County, CA (Asking Price up to $2M)

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-09

**Method note (please read first):** The research environment's network proxy blocked direct page fetches of every listing marketplace tried: BizBuySell, BizQuest, BusinessBroker.net, Sunbelt Network, DealStream and BizBen all returned EGRESS_BLOCKED. All data below therefore comes from **web-search result extracts** of those marketplace pages, not from opening individual listings. As a result:
- **Individual listing URLs could not be retrieved.** Each "Source URL" below is the marketplace category or search page where the listing appeared, so a reader has to find the listing on that page.
- **Listing dates are not disclosed** in the extracts. The "Seen" date for every row is 2026-10-09, the date it showed up in search, and does **not** confirm that the listing is still active. Search snippets flagged some Sunbelt pages as roughly 1,600–1,700 days old (about 2021–2022), and some BizBen auto listings as dating from 2023–2024. Treat those rows as possibly stale.
- **Some price-to-listing pairings are uncertain.** The search tool noted that scraped page text sometimes made it unclear which price went with which listing. Those rows are marked "(pairing uncertain)".
- **Cash flow type:** Unless a row says "SDE" or "Adj. EBITDA", the listing reported only "cash flow", which on BizBuySell/BizQuest-style sites normally means seller's discretionary earnings (SDE). That is an inference, not something stated in each listing.
- **Not disclosed in any extract:** year established (except where noted) and whether real estate is included (except where noted). Neither was visible for most rows.
- The multiple is asking price divided by cash flow. I calculated it myself.

## What specific businesses are for sale in LA and Orange County under $2M, with full financials?

### Takeaway
I compiled 41 LA/OC listings with asking prices of $2M or less from search extracts of the major marketplaces. Of these, 9 disclose asking price, revenue and cash flow together. Most others give price and cash flow but not revenue. The listings span restaurants and franchises (most common), auto repair, manufacturing and distribution, dental and healthcare, home services, laundromats and retail. Asking price / cash flow multiples are mostly between 1.0x and 4.0x, with a few outliers.
