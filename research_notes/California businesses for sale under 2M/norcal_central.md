# Businesses for Sale ≤ $2M — Sacramento, Central Valley, Central Coast, Northern California (non-Bay Area)

**Method and caveats (read first):** Research done 2026-10-09. I could not open any marketplace directly: BizBuySell, BizQuest, BizBen, BusinessBroker.net, DealStream, BizTrader and Sunbelt all returned "EGRESS_BLOCKED" from the network proxy. Every figure below comes from search-engine extraction of those marketplaces' city, county and category result pages (plus one BizQuest listing URL). As a result:
- The **source URL is usually the regional or category results page where the listing appeared**, not the listing's own page. Exceptions are noted.
- **Original listing dates were not visible.** "Seen" means it appeared in search results on 2026-10-09. Search indexes can lag, so some listings may already be sold or under contract. One search summary called several results possibly stale, and one BizQuest result was flagged as about 190 days old.
- "Cash flow" on these sites is almost always seller-reported **SDE (seller's discretionary earnings)**, unless the listing says EBITDA or net income. None of the figures are verified.
- The multiple is asking price ÷ cash flow, which I computed. Not disclosed = ND.

## What specific businesses are for sale in these regions under $2M, with full financials?

### Takeaway
I collected about 55 in-region listings at or under $2M from marketplace results pages. About 22 disclose asking price, revenue and cash flow, and about 25 more disclose price and cash flow. They cover all four price bands and most target industries. Small owner-operated service and restaurant businesses mostly ask 1.5–3.5x SDE. That matches BizBuySell's Sacramento-page average asking earnings multiple of about 2.6x.

### Inferences
- This geography is dominated by small owner-operator deals. **No in-region listing priced at $1.25M–$2M with disclosed financials turned up in search results.** Larger deals tend to jump above $2M, usually because real estate, a gas station or cannabis is included. The report writer should call that band thin here, not merely under-sampled.
- The multiples I could compute (about 35 listings) cluster at **1.6x–3.5x SDE**, with a median around 2.5x. That agrees with BizBuySell's 2.6x Sacramento average.

### Gaps
- No individual listing page could be opened, so I could not confirm current active status, listing dates, years established (mostly ND), real estate inclusion, or whether "cash flow" is SDE or EBITDA.
- Few Redding/Shasta and Tahoe listings, and no Merced, Visalia/Tulare or Yuba listings, carried full financials in the snippets.
- Search snippets sometimes mix figures between neighboring listings on results pages, especially #6 and #16. Each number should be checked on the live listing.

## Which listings look like strong value (low multiple, high cash flow relative to price)?

### Inferences
- FedEx Ground routes usually trade at low SDE multiples because of contract risk with FedEx, vehicle capex and labor costs. The low multiple partly prices that risk in; it is not pure value.
- The Stockton gas station plus real estate at $450K is the most anomalous "value" deal. Either the land is small or rural, or there are environmental or underground-tank issues. It needs Phase I diligence.
- Sub-1x listings (Modesto rim repair at 0.87x, SLO mobile auto interior at 0.88x, Tahoe/Reno home franchise at 0.99x) almost certainly count the owner's labor as cash flow. The buyer is effectively buying a job.
