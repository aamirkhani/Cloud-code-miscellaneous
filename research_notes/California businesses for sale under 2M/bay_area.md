**Read this first (method and limits).** Research date: 2026-10-09. The egress proxy blocked direct access to every listing site tried (BizBuySell, BizQuest, DealStream, BizTrader, BusinessBroker.net, BizBen, MergerPlace, LoopNet, Sunbelt, Transworld, VR, Murphy, The Bay Advisors, BusinessesForSale.com). All figures below come from web-search index snippets of those pages, retrieved 2026-10-09. As a result:
- **I could not confirm any listing was still active on 2026-10-09.** Search snippets carry no listing dates, and some underlying pages are known to be older. Treat every row as "seen in search index 2026-10-09, status unverified."
- "Cash flow" is the figure BizBuySell-style sites label "Cash Flow." That is usually SDE, but brokers define it differently. Where a source said EBITDA or SDE explicitly, I note it.
- Year established and real-estate inclusion were rarely shown in snippets. They are marked "not disclosed" (ND) unless the snippet stated them.
- The search tool summarizes pages, and it can mix up figures between neighboring listings. I flag one suspected case (#11).
- Multiple = asking price ÷ cash flow, rounded to 2 decimals.

## What specific businesses are for sale in the Bay Area under $2M, with full financials?

### Takeaway
I compiled 45 Bay Area listings with asking prices ≤ $2M from search snippets, covering all 9 counties and every price band. About 15 disclose asking price, revenue and cash flow together. Most others show price and cash flow only. Restaurants, services, routes (FedEx, food distribution), healthcare and trades dominate. E-commerce and tech listings are thin; only one Bay Area IT/MSP listing with full financials was found.

### Inferences
- Coverage by price band: $0-250K has about 10 rows, $250-750K about 24, $750K-1.25M 6, and $1.25-2M 6. The $750K-2M bands are dominated by FedEx routes, contractors and one manufacturer.

- The Bay Area has very few public e-commerce or tech-services listings under $2M with financials. Buyers in that niche would likely need national marketplaces such as Flippa, or direct broker relationships.

- No Bay Area e-commerce listing with financials was found. Healthcare is limited to dental and medical practices. No Bay Area franchise resale with full financials in the $750K-2M band was found.

## Which listings look like strong value (low multiple, high cash flow relative to price)?

### Takeaway
On reported numbers, the standouts are S&S Welding, San Jose (0.93x), the SF dental practice (0.82x), the Vallejo Mission Foods route (1.37x), the SF pizza restaurant (1.25x), the Vallejo locksmith (1.48x), the Fremont auto repair shops (~1.6x), the Marin plumbing contractor (1.76x), and the Marin salon/beauty school (1.84x). Sub-1.5x multiples usually mean owner-labor-dependent SDE or data problems, so verify them first.

### Takeaway
The main red flags are implausible cash flow-to-price ratios (the Vacaville pool route, the SF dental practice), missing cash flow on restaurants with high stated revenue, low-margin or high-multiple listings, figures that conflict between sites, and stale or pending listings still shown in search results. No declining-revenue data was visible. Real estate inflated price in at least one listing just over the cap.

### Cited Findings
- Vacaville pool service route: $125K ask vs $468K claimed CF (0.27x). Almost certainly a data error, or revenue mislabeled as cash flow. — [BizBuySell Solano](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/solano-county-businesses-for-sale/)
- SF dental practice: cash flow exceeds asking price ($480K vs $395K), which the source itself called unusual. — [BizBuySell Bay Area health care](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-francisco-bay-area/health-care-and-fitness-businesses-for-sale/)
- Revenue shown with no cash flow: Alameda restaurant ($250K ask, $1.18M revenue) and Walnut Creek restaurant/bar ($395K ask, $2.1M revenue). — [BizBuySell Alameda Co.](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/alameda-county-businesses-for-sale/), [BizBuySell Walnut Creek](https://m.bizbuysell.com/california/walnut-creek-businesses-for-sale/)
- Thin margins: San Mateo freight forwarder, 4.2% CF margin ($81,557 on $1.95M). Santa Rosa moving & storage, 8.4% margin ($210,597 on $2.49M) at a 4.0x multiple. — [BizBuySell San Mateo Co.](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-mateo-county-businesses-for-sale/), [BizBuySell Santa Rosa](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/santa-rosa-businesses-for-sale/)
- High multiple: SF grocery market at 6.61x EBITDA ($694,148 / $105K). — [BizBuySell Bay Area health care page](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-francisco-bay-area/health-care-and-fitness-businesses-for-sale/)
- Conflicting figures: the Sonoma electrical contractor appears at $1.29M/$400K and at $1.375M/$423.7K. An Alameda Co. GMP supplement manufacturer shows cash flow of $90K, $275K, $800K and $335K across sites. — [BizBuySell Sonoma](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/sonoma-county-businesses-for-sale/), [BizBuySell Bay Area manufacturing](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-francisco-bay-area/manufacturing-businesses-for-sale/)
- Real estate inflating price: a European specialty auto shop is $575K for the business plus $1.6M for real estate. — [BizBuySell Bay Area automotive](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-francisco-bay-area/automotive-and-boat-businesses-for-sale/)
- Stale or pending listings still indexed: several FedEx routes are sold, the IT/MSP is offer pending, the Marin tax practice is sale pending, and some auto-shop posts date from 2023. — sources as cited in the tables above
- Fairfield FedEx package: $850K cash required but a $1.7M total purchase price with financing. The headline asking price can understate the total consideration. — [BizBuySell Solano](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/solano-county-businesses-for-sale/)

### Inferences
- FedEx route valuations depend heavily on fleet condition and contract renewal dates. Comparing EBITDA-labeled listings with cash flow-labeled ones overstates the differences.
- Restaurant listings that show revenue but hide cash flow (#13, #24) are likely low-margin or asset-value sales.

### Gaps
- No multi-year revenue trends were visible, so declining revenue could not be assessed for any listing.
- Real-estate inclusion is unknown for nearly all rows.
