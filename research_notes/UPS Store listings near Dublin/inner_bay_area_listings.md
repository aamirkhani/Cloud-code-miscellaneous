# The UPS Store and comparable pack-and-ship listings for sale in Alameda, Contra Costa, San Francisco, San Mateo and Santa Clara counties (seen 2026-10-10)

How I gathered this: direct fetches of bizbuysell.com, bizben.com and theupsstorefranchise.com were blocked. The WebFetch tool returned DNS errors (ENOTFOUND), and curl got a 403 from the egress proxy (connect_rejected). Every data point below therefore comes from **search-engine snippets** of those pages, plus broker pages that are indexed. All figures are what the sellers report in the snippets. None have been verified, and every one should be checked with the broker. "Date seen" for every row is 2026-10-10, the date of the search snippet. It is not the listing's posting date unless the row says otherwise.
