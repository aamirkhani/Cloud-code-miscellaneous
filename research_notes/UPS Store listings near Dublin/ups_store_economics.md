## 1. FDD data (2025/2026): fees, term, remodels, Item 19

### Takeaway
Ongoing fees total roughly 8.5% of adjusted sales (5% royalty + 1% marketing + 2.5% national ad, the ad fee capped) plus a ~$2.4K-$2.9K/yr tech fee. Item 19 (2026 FDD) reports 2025 average adjusted gross sales of about $724K and a median of about $693K for ~5,058 traditional centers, with wide dispersion. Note that gross sales include pass-through UPS shipping charges. A transfer can trigger a mandatory Laser Lite remodel ($97K-$281K) if the seller's term is close to expiring, which is the biggest hidden cost in a resale.

### Cited Findings
**Fees**
- 2026 FDD (Traditional Centers): 5% royalty, 1% "The UPS Store marketing fee", 2.5% national advertising fee capped at $27,734/yr, annual technology fee $2,868. No minimum local ad spend required. — [FranchiseEvidence (2026 FDD summary)](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)
- 2025 FDD per another aggregator: 5% royalty on "Subject to Royalty" (STR) sales, 1% of STR marketing fee, 2.5% of STR national ad fee. Franchise fee $29,950 first unit / $19,950 additional. — [FranchiseChatter 2025 review](https://www.franchisechatter.com/2025/11/11/the-ups-store-franchise-review-2025-costs-fees-news-average-revenues-and-or-profits/) (via search summary)
- Conflicting: a 2026 summary lists a $39,950 initial franchise fee. Some sites describe total ongoing fees as 8.5% (5% + 3.5%), while FDDIQ reportedly says 7.5% total. — [Franchise search summaries: worldreview1989](https://www.worldreview1989.com/2026/08/The-UPS-Store-Franchise-Cost.html); [FDDIQ](https://fddiq.com/blog/the-ups-store-franchise-cost-profit); [Franchise Payback](https://www.franchisepayback.com/franchise/the-ups-store)
- Technology fee: 2025 FDD "Annual Technology Development and Support Fee (New Franchise and Transfers)" $2,418, billed monthly. 2026 FDD $2,868/yr ($2,748 rural/second franchise). One-time proprietary software license $4,750 for a new franchise. — [Scribd: The UPS Store Inc. (Traditional) FDD 2025](https://www.scribd.com/document/926665318/The-UPS-Store-Inc-Traditional-FDD-2025); [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)
- Center Development Fee (2025 FDD): $7,500 for new centers, renewals, **transfers** and Laser Lite remodels, or 20% of local labor costs for upgrades smaller than a Laser Lite. — [Scribd FDD 2025](https://www.scribd.com/document/926665318/The-UPS-Store-Inc-Traditional-FDD-2025); [Lopes Law: Item 6 Other Fees](https://lopeslawllc.com/ups-store-fdd-item-6-other-fees/)
- New center investment (2026 FDD): $222K-$606K excluding real estate. — [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)

**Term, renewal, transfer fees**
- Term 10 years, renewable for successive 10-year terms under the then-current agreement. — [Francloud](https://www.francloud.com/franchises/the-ups-store)
- Renewal fee 25% of the then-current initial franchise fee. Renewal notice is due no later than 6 months before term end. — [FranchiseChatter fee list](https://www.franchisechatter.com/2018/02/11/considering-a-ups-store-franchise-dont-overlook-these-25-important-franchise-fees/) (blog. Original article is 2018 and possibly updated, so verify)
- Transfer (2026 FDD summary): transfer fee $6,000 (franchisor may raise it). Separate processing fee $6,000 (reduced to $1,500 if a finder's fee is paid to the franchisor). Pro-rated renewal fee due on transfer. Family transfer fee $1,750. Franchisor holds a right of first refusal. — [FranchiseEvidence / search summary](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/); [Lopes Law Item 6](https://lopeslawllc.com/ups-store-fdd-item-6-other-fees/)

**Remodel / store refresh**
- 2025 FDD: "If the transfer date is within 3.5 years of the expiration date of your seller's then-existing franchise term, you must complete a full Laser Lite design remodel within 9 months of the transfer date." A secondary summary says "generally required within 11 months of transfer," which conflicts and may reflect a different FDD year. The 2025 FDD also says existing franchisees who missed the extended renewal-remodel timeline must remodel to Laser Lite. — [Scribd FDD 2025](https://www.scribd.com/document/926665318/The-UPS-Store-Inc-Traditional-FDD-2025); [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)
- 2026 FDD Item 7-style estimate for "Remodel Traditional Center (Laser Lite, non-Rural)": $97,047-$281,271. — [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)

**Item 19**
- 2026 FDD: average annual adjusted gross sales $724,293 (calendar 2025) across 5,058 franchised U.S. Traditional Centers open a full year. Prior years: $719,842 (2024) and $721,245 (2023). 2025 median $692,858. Only 45% of centers exceeded the average. Top 10% averaged $1,248,208 and bottom 10% averaged $345,790. — [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)
- The gross sales definition includes UPS shipping charges collected from customers, so this is a revenue figure inflated by pass-through carrier costs. — [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)
- Conflicts: FDDIQ lists a 2024 median of $686,743. Another site lists a 2025 median of ~$687K from 4,931 units, and franchiseinvestordata cites an AUV of ~$692K with a top quartile of $950K and a bottom quartile of $400K. Two sites claim there is no Item 19 at all, which contradicts the others and is likely outdated. — [FDDIQ](https://fddiq.com/blog/the-ups-store-franchise-cost-profit); [Franchise Investor Data](https://franchiseinvestordata.com/franchise/ups-store); [Franchise Clues](https://www.franchiseclues.com/industry/shipping-packaging-franchises)
- System: 3,293 franchisees, 883 of them multi-unit operators (2026 Multi-Unit 50). — [Franchising.com Multi-Unit 50 2026](https://franchising.com/articles/20260829_the_2026_multiunit_50_a_snapshot_of_scale.html)
- Company-owned centers rose to 16 (from 2 in 2023), mostly from reacquiring Texas centers in 2024. The franchise is tied to a separate UPS Contract Carrier Agreement, and termination of that agreement automatically ends the franchise. — [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)

### Inferences
- All-in ongoing franchisor take is about 8.5% of royalty-bearing sales plus ~$2.9K/yr tech. On a median ~$693K store that is roughly $55K-$60K/yr, though the ad cap and the STR definition may reduce it. The exact STR base (which may exclude some shipping) needs FDD verification.
- The remodel-on-transfer trigger (seller's term within 3.5 years of expiry) is decisive for valuation. A listing whose term expires soon effectively carries a $100K-$280K capex liability plus the $7,500 development fee.

### Gaps
- Could not read the FDD PDF directly, so the exact Item 19 tables (by region or California), the STR definition and the ad-fee cap details are not verified first-hand.
- No mailbox-revenue share or service mix breakdown found in any Item 19 summary.
- Franchise fee conflict ($29,950 vs $39,950) is unresolved. It matters little to a resale buyer except through the renewal fee, which is 25% of the then-current fee.

## 2. Resale valuation: SDE and revenue multiples, typical margins

### Takeaway
Broker and guide commentary places UPS Store resales at roughly 2.5-4x SDE (wider quoted range 1.5-4.5x), with lower multiples where a remodel is due. California listings in 2024-26 show asking prices of roughly 2.3-3.5x claimed cash flow and roughly 0.5-1.2x revenue. Reported SDE margins span ~12% (estimate) to 30%+ (seller claims). No verified BizBuySell UPS-specific closed-deal multiple was found.

### Cited Findings
- A buy-side broker quotes 3.5-5x EBITDA as typical for UPS Store and similar retail-service franchises, and 3-4.5x SDE for SBA-financed single units. Stores that don't need an upgrade get higher multiples, and stores where the buyer must complete one get lower multiples. — [CT Acquisitions: Franchise Business Valuation 2026](https://ctacquisitions.com/franchise-business-valuation/)
- General franchise-resale guides: 2.0-3.5x SDE single-unit, 3.0-4.5x multi-unit. 2.5-3.5x for stable single units. — [RevenueLab calculator](https://www.revenuelab.fyi/franchise-resale-value-calculator); [FranchiseVS resale guide](https://franchisevs.com/guide/franchise-resale-valuation-guide); [franbase.ai](https://franbase.ai/guides/how-to-value-a-franchise-resale)
- Older Q&A (undated, likely pre-2024): 1.5-3x SDE for a UPS Store. — [JustAnswer](https://www.justanswer.com/finance/mad8m-multiple-given-ups-store-looking.html)
- Broker fees typically run 8-12%. — [FranchiseVS](https://franchisevs.com/guide/franchise-resale-valuation-guide)
- Listing example: two remodeled UPS Stores with $1.38M revenue, weighted adjusted SDE $207,482, asking $989,000, which implies ~4.8x SDE and ~0.72x revenue (asking, not sold). — [BizQuest listing](https://www.bizquest.com/business-for-sale/two-remodeled-ups-store-franchise-1-38m-revenue/BW2480345)
- California listings (asking, from search snippets, dates partly unclear):
  - Alameda County: $849K-$859K asking (two figures shown), cash flow $262,363 (2024), which implies ~3.2-3.3x. — [BizBuySell CA UPS listings](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/ups-stores-for-sale/)
  - Napa County: $699K asking, cash flow $222,102, which implies ~3.1x. — [BizBuySell CA](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/ups-stores-for-sale/)
  - Santa Ana: $200K asking, SDE $88,795, which implies ~2.25x. — [BizBuySell Santa Ana listing](https://www.bizbuysell.com/business-opportunity/the-ups-store-franchise-in-prime-location/2434847/)
  - Sacramento: $310K asking on $430K revenue (~0.72x revenue), SDE not disclosed. — [BizBuySell Sacramento](https://www.bizbuysell.com/business-opportunity/the-ups-store-for-sale/2245335/)
  - Salinas: $412K asking on $467K revenue (~0.88x revenue). The page appears stale (lease expiration 2022). — [BizBuySell Salinas](https://www.bizbuysell.com/Business-Opportunity/The-UPS-Store-For-Sale-in-Salinas-California/1739918/)
  - Oxnard: $329K asking, cash flow shown as $115K on one page and $250K on another (conflicting). — [BizQuest CA](https://www.bizquest.com/ups-stores-for-sale-in-california/)
- Margin evidence: FDDIQ's estimate of ~12% EBITDA margin gives ~$82K on the $686,743 median (an assumption, not reported data). One review cites typical owner earnings of $60K-$120K/yr. Franchiseinvestordata estimates ~$110K using an assumed 15.9% category margin. — [FDDIQ](https://fddiq.com/blog/the-ups-store-franchise-cost-profit); [The Franchise Insiders](https://www.thefranchiseinsiders.com/blog/ups-store-franchise-cost-profit-review-2026); [Franchise Investor Data](https://franchiseinvestordata.com/franchise/ups-store)
- Seller-claimed margins on listings: Raleigh-area store with SDE $280,558 on $797,370 revenue (35%). Las Vegas store with SDE $146,000 (30.48% margin). — [BizQuest Raleigh](https://www.bizquest.com/business-for-sale/greater-raleigh-area-profitable-ups-store-under-contract/BW2482878); search summary of BizQuest listings

### Inferences
- A reasonable benchmark band for a Bay Area single unit is ~2.5-3.5x verified SDE, adjusted downward for an imminent remodel or term expiry and for SDE that assumes owner labor. Listed SDE margins of 30%+ usually treat the owner's full-time labor as profit. An absentee buyer should deduct a manager's salary (~$60K-$80K loaded in the Bay Area, my estimate, not sourced), which can cut SDE by a third or more.
- Revenue multiples of ~0.5-1.2x appear in CA listings. Because gross sales include pass-through shipping, revenue multiples are a weak metric. Use SDE after manager salary.

### Gaps
- BizBuySell Insight/closed-deal data for UPS Stores specifically was not accessible (bizbuysell.com blocked). No verified median sale-to-ask ratio or closed multiple.
- Listing dates and current status are uncertain for the CA examples.

## 3. Revenue mix and trends (shipping, mailbox, print, notary, Amazon, UPS changes, disputes)

### Takeaway
Profit is driven by print, mailbox and services rather than retail shipping, which owners describe as low-margin. Free Amazon/Happy Returns drop-offs bring traffic but are reportedly a concern for some owners. UPS's plan to cut Amazon volume by more than 50% by H2 2026 (announced Jan 2025) is a parent-network change with unquantified store-level impact. No 2023-2026 franchisee lawsuits or association disputes were found.

### Cited Findings
- A former owner (Glassdoor) says the "profit margin of shipping is poor," so a store has to function as a print shop to make money. A franchisee blog cites constraints on suppliers and signage that limit cost-cutting. — [Glassdoor owner reviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/The-UPS-Store-Owner-Reviews-EI_IE307820.0,13_KO14,19.htm); [Prosperopedia owner account](https://prosperopedia.com/buying-owning-ups-store-franchise-review/) (age unclear)
- UPS agreed (Oct 2023) to buy Happy Returns from PayPal for box-free, label-free returns, combining its network with ~5,200 UPS Store locations. — [BusinessWire Oct 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20231025012177/en); [Wikipedia: Happy Returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Returns_(company))
- Happy Returns expanded to retailers such as Shein (2024). — [Fortune Aug 2024](https://www.fortune.com/2024/08/21/ups-happy-returns-shein-buy-online-return-in-store)
- UPS Q4 2024 earnings (Jan 2025): UPS will reduce Amazon volume by more than 50% by H2 2026. Amazon was 11.8% of UPS revenue in 2024 and ~20-25% of U.S. network volume. CEO Carol Tomé said Amazon is "our largest customer, but it's not our most profitable customer." The plan was tied to ~$1B of cost savings, labor cuts and facility closures. — [Supply Chain Dive](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/ups-amazon-volume-reduction-q4-2024-earnings/738749/); [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ups-slashing-amazon-volume-by-50-in-push-for-profitability)
- Aggregator claims (unverified): reduced footfall and prepaid-label volume at stores. "No net unit growth anticipated" in 2026. Anecdotal reports of franchisees declining renewal. — [Franchise Investor Data](https://franchiseinvestordata.com/franchise/ups-store)
- A 2026 owner-discussion summary says some participants feel the model has become overly dependent on free Amazon returns (explicitly not statistical). — [worldreview1989 2026](https://www.worldreview1989.com/2026/08/The-UPS-Store-Franchise-Cost.html)
- The only franchisee lawsuit found is a historical one (c. 2003) over the conversion of Mail Boxes Etc. to The UPS Store brand. Nothing comparable surfaced for 2023-2026. — [Post & Parcel](https://postandparcel.info/8173/news/franchisees-sue-ups-over-conversion-to-ups-store/)

### Inferences
- Item 19 averages flat at ~$720K for 2023-2025 suggest roughly flat nominal sales, meaning real declines after inflation, while wage and rent costs rose.
- Amazon returns are free to the consumer. Whatever per-package compensation the franchisee receives was not found, so returns likely add traffic and labor more than margin. A buyer should ask the seller for a P&L split by category, including returns counts and compensation.

### Gaps
- No verified data on mailbox, print or notary revenue share, or on per-return compensation to franchisees.
- No primary source on UPS Store retail shipping-margin changes or on 2025-26 UPS retail rate changes as they affect franchisee markup.
- No information found on any active independent franchisee association activity in 2023-2026 (not searched exhaustively).

## 4. Transfer process, training, absentee ownership, multi-unit norms

### Takeaway
Transfers require franchisor approval, fees (~$6K transfer plus ~$6K processing plus a pro-rated renewal fee plus a $7,500 development fee) and buyer training, and are subject to a franchisor right of first refusal. Absentee ownership is permitted, but each center must have an on-premises "primary operator" who has completed the franchisee training. Multi-unit ownership is common (883 of 3,293 franchisees).

### Cited Findings
- Entrepreneur directory: absentee ownership allowed = "Yes." The center's day-to-day operations must at all times be directly supervised by an on-premises "primary operator," and all primary operators must complete the new franchisee training program and a financial management training program. — [Entrepreneur franchise directory](https://www.entrepreneur.com/franchises/directory/ups-store-the/282553); [Franchise Direct](https://www.franchisedirect.com/mailingshippingfranchises/the-ups-store-franchise-05563/ufoc/)
- Contrary view: one review says the model is an owner-operated business that is not semi-absentee or passive. A data site calls it "absentee owner friendly" yet cites 40-50 hrs/week. — [The Franchise Insiders 2026](https://www.thefranchiseinsiders.com/blog/ups-store-franchise-cost-profit-review-2026); [Franchise Investor Data](https://franchiseinvestordata.com/franchise/ups-store)
- Training: online tutorials, two in-store sessions at a certified training center, a print services course, and a business course at the franchisor's training university. — [The UPS Store Franchise: Training](https://www.theupsstorefranchise.com/how-it-works/franchise-training)
- Resale listings advertise absentee-run stores with "seasoned staff" (seller marketing). — [BizQuest listing](https://www.bizquest.com/business-for-sale/ups-store-with-seasoned-staff/BW2263739/)
- Multi-unit: 883 multi-unit operators out of 3,293 franchisees (2026). — [Franchising.com](https://franchising.com/articles/20260829_the_2026_multiunit_50_a_snapshot_of_scale.html)
- Right of first refusal, plus transfer and processing fees. — [FranchiseEvidence](https://franchiseevidence.com/franchise/the-ups-store/)

### Inferences
- Semi-absentee ownership is feasible only with a trained, on-site primary operator (the owner or a manager the owner pays to complete training). Economics should be modeled with a full manager salary. Single-unit absentee ownership is thin at median volumes, so multi-unit or above-median stores make the model more workable.

### Gaps
- Exact Item 17 transfer conditions (general release, buyer net-worth/liquidity tests, training timeline before closing) and whether the primary operator must hold an ownership stake were not verified from the FDD text.
- The term "Certified Center Manager" was not found in any source.

## 5. Financing (SBA and Sharia-compliant options)

### Takeaway
The SBA Franchise Directory was reinstated June 1, 2025 (current file effective April 17, 2026). Lender sites say The UPS Store is listed, which lets lenders skip an addendum review, though I did not verify this in the SBA file. For interest-free financing, practical options are seller financing structured as profit-sharing or as a fixed-price installment sale, plus a few US Islamic finance providers (UIF, which absorbed LARIBA in April 2026, and Bank of Whittier's riba-free business financing). No confirmed provider offers franchise acquisition financing specifically.

### Cited Findings
- SBA Franchise Directory suspended May 2023, restored June 1, 2025. Lenders can rely on it, and an SBA addendum is not required for listed brands. — [Foley, May 2025](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2025/05/sba-franchise-directory-reinstated-easing-loan-access-franchisees/); [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2025/06/the-sba-franchise-directory-is-back)
- SBA support page lists a directory spreadsheet effective April 17, 2026. — [SBA Franchise Directory](https://www.sba.gov/document/support-sba-franchise-directory)
- Commercial lender site states The UPS Store is on the SBA Franchise Directory (not verified against SBA's file). — [ClearValue Lending](https://clearvaluelending.com/franchises/ups-store/financing)
- Broker: SBA-financed UPS Store single units trade at 3-4.5x SDE. — [CT Acquisitions](https://ctacquisitions.com/franchise-business-valuation/)
- Islamic structures: murabaha (financier buys the asset and resells it at a disclosed markup in installments) and musharaka (joint ownership/partnership). — [Swoop Funding guide](https://swoopfunding.com/us/business-loans/islamic-business-finance/); [Wikipedia: Profit and loss sharing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Profit_and_loss_sharing)
- Providers (third-party comparison site, which itself says it does not certify products): UIF (musharaka-based), Bank of Whittier N.A. ("RF Business & Commercial Financing," riba-free). LARIBA reportedly merged into UIF on April 1, 2026. Bank of Whittier reported $152.6M in assets as of June 30, 2026. — [HalalWallet business financing](https://www.halalwallet.us/business-financing); [HalalWallet: Bank of Whittier review](https://www.halalwallet.us/providers/bank-of-whittier); [HalalWallet: LARIBA review](https://www.halalwallet.us/blog/lariba-home-financing-review)
- LARIBA historically financed small and medium businesses using ijara (leasing) and musharaka structures. — [Mohammed Amin on LARIBA](https://www.mohammedamin.com/Islamic_finance/American-Finance-House-Lariba.html)
- HalalWallet lists a Bay Area (San Francisco) halal business financing page. — [HalalWallet SF](https://www.halalwallet.us/business-financing/ca/san-francisco)
- Bank of Whittier's site carries a scam alert saying it has no reps outside the USA, so verify via FDIC/OCC before engaging. — [Bank of Whittier LARIBA model page](http://www.bankofwhittier.com/lariba-model)

### Inferences
- Interest-free paths for a resale: (a) a seller note restructured as a higher fixed purchase price paid in installments, with no interest and no late-fee interest; (b) an earn-out or profit-share (musharaka-style) with the seller; (c) equity partners; (d) UIF or Bank of Whittier commercial financing, if they will underwrite goodwill-heavy franchise purchases (unconfirmed). Seller-financing appetite and franchisor consent to seller liens must be confirmed. A buyer should get a qualified scholar to review the specific contract.

### Gaps
- Not verified directly in the SBA spreadsheet that The UPS Store is listed.
- No confirmation that any US Islamic finance provider finances franchise or goodwill acquisitions. No Sharia-compliant SBA-guaranteed product was confirmed.
- No data on typical lender appetite, down payment or DSCR for UPS Store resales in 2025-26 beyond broker comments.

## 6. California-specific costs (minimum wage, Bay Area retail rents)

### Takeaway
The 2026 statewide minimum wage is $16.90/hr, and many Bay Area cities are higher. East Bay retail asking rents average roughly $32-$34/SF/yr. A typical 1,200-1,500 SF UPS Store would pay roughly $40K-$50K base rent plus NNN, which is my computation, not a sourced figure.

### Cited Findings
- California minimum wage is $16.90/hr from Jan 1, 2026 (up 2.49% from $16.50). The exempt salary floor is $70,304/yr. More than 40 localities set higher rates. — [CalChamber HRWatchdog](https://hrwatchdog.calchamber.com/2025/08/californias-2026-minimum-wage-increases-to-16-90-per-hour/); [Davis Wright Tremaine](https://www.dwt.com/blogs/employment-labor-and-benefits/2025/12/california-minimum-wage-state-and-local-increases); [Fisher Phillips](https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/news-insights/california-minimum-wage-will-increase-in-2026.html)
- The $70,304 exempt floor matters for a salaried manager. An exempt store manager in CA must earn at least this amount. — [DWT](https://www.dwt.com/blogs/employment-labor-and-benefits/2025/12/california-minimum-wage-state-and-local-increases)
- East Bay average retail asking rent is ~$32.32-$32.92/SF (listing-based). Oakland averages ~$32/SF. — [CommercialCafe East Bay](https://www.commercialcafe.com/retail/us/ca/east-bay/); [CommercialSearch](https://www.commercialsearch.com/retail/us/ca/east-bay/); [CityFeet Oakland](https://www.cityfeet.com/cont/oakland-ca/retail-space-for-lease)
- NAI Northern California East Bay retail report: strip center asking rent $28.90/SF, with a 2026 strip-center row at $34.10/SF (likely a forecast. The report dates from ~mid-2024). — [NAI NorCal East Bay Retail (2024)](https://nainorcal.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/East-Bay-CA-USA-Retail-Market.pdf)
- National strip/neighborhood inline benchmark is $18-$28/SF NNN (not Bay Area). — [TenantBase 2026](https://blog.tenantbase.com/how-much-does-retail-space-cost-in-2026)

### Inferences
- For an absentee owner, the CA exempt-salary floor ($70,304) sets a hard minimum cost for a salaried manager. With payroll taxes this is ~$76K+ (estimate). On a ~$700K-revenue store this alone can consume most of a 12-15% EBITDA margin. Absentee ownership pencils out mainly at above-average-volume stores (e.g., $1M+), or with an hourly non-exempt lead plus owner oversight.

### Gaps
- No Tri-Valley (Dublin/Pleasanton/San Ramon) specific 2026 strip-center rent data found. Need a CBRE, Colliers, Cushman or NAI quarterly report or a LoopNet search.
- Local minimum wages for Dublin, Pleasanton and San Ramon were not checked. Dublin and Pleasanton are not known to me to have local ordinances, so verify.
