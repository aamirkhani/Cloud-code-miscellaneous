## Which UPS Store locations are for sale in these areas now, and with what financials?

### Takeaway
Only **Napa County** has a UPS Store listing with full financials: $699K asking, $222,102 SDE, $760,197 revenue. That is about **3.1x SDE** and about **4.1x EBITDA**, and it may be pending. **Sonoma/Marin** has a single seller offering several centers. Per-store asking prices run $470K–$540K, and the bundle listing carries a $630K price next to $196,066 cash flow, though the snippets are inconsistent. **Sacramento** has one or two franchisor-posted resales; one asks $310K on $430K revenue, cash flow not disclosed, and its lease date has already passed. I found **no current UPS Store resale** in Solano, San Joaquin (Stockton/Tracy/Manteca/Lodi), Stanislaus (Modesto/Turlock), Santa Cruz, Yolo or Placer.
