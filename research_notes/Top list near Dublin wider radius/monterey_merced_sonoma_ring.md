Date of research: 2026-10-13.

**How the data was gathered:** Every direct fetch to BizBuySell, BizBen and BizQuest failed with DNS errors (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`). All rows below come from **web-search snippets** of listing pages, broker sites and syndicated copies (BizQuest, BusinessBroker.net, LoopNet.ca, BizBen, DealStream). I did not open the individual listing pages. Snippets almost never show posting dates, so most rows are "Active? (unverified)".

**Dedup:** Rows were checked against the five prior notes files. I dropped these because they are already covered: the Sonoma Mission Foods route ($120K/$95,346), the Windsor 13-, 15- and 16-route FedEx packages, the Santa Rosa tanning, dental, moving and day-spa listings, the Hollister pool route, bike shop, RV-accessory maker, spice blender, vet hospital, UPS store and carnitas, the Lodi tax/CPA practices, the Merced/Modesto 100+ Speed Queen $1.7M laundromat, the Turlock two-restaurant bundle, and the Oakdale restaurant.

"ND" means not disclosed in the source. CF means seller-stated cash flow (SDE unless marked EBITDA).

## Q1. Which new listings in the ring suit an absentee or semi-absentee buyer and pass the buyer's filters?

### Takeaway
I found 35 new rows that pass the filters (no alcohol, tobacco, cannabis, lottery, pork-heavy food or licensed-owner professions). The strongest absentee fits are the **Salinas 7-route FedEx package** ($625K / CF $290.5K, full-time manager in place), the **Windsor 6-route FedEx package** ($875K / CF $278.8K), the **UPS Store "near Monterey County"** ($1.2M / CF $312K, 8 employees), and the **Monterey County B2B print/promo company** ($1.15M / CF $353K). Merced/Stanislaus/Lodi is thin and mostly small owner-run routes. No self-storage, staffing or pharmacy listing ≤ $2M surfaced in the ring.
