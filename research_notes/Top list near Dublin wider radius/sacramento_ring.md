**Method and access caveat (read first):** Every direct fetch failed on 2026-10-13. WebFetch returned DNS errors (ENOTFOUND) for bizbuysell.com and bizben.com. curl got proxy `CONNECT 403` (organization policy) for bizbuysell.com, bizquest.com and dealstream.com. **Every row below therefore comes from search-engine snippets of marketplace category pages or listing pages, not from opened listings.**

What that means for the data:
- Listing IDs are mostly ND.
- Broker names are mostly ND.
- Revenue, rent, employee counts and real-estate status are often ND.
- The "date seen" for every row is 2026-10-13, meaning the date of the snippet. It is not the posting date.
- Snippets carried no reliable posting dates, so status is "Active? (unverified)" unless a snippet said otherwise.
- Sunbelt Sacramento pages were flagged by the search tool as cached roughly 1,050–1,734 days ago, so they are likely stale.
- Realmo pages were about 500 days old.

**Deduplication:** I checked against `norcal_central.md`, `valley_santa_cruz.md`, `absentee_categories.md` and `outer_ring_pharmacies.md`. Listings already in those notes are excluded or explicitly flagged as possible duplicates or price changes.

## Which listings in the Sacramento ring (≤ $2M, not already noted) suit an absentee or semi-absentee buyer?

### Takeaway
I logged 46 new rows; the exclusions are listed separately. About 28 have both price and cash flow. The strongest absentee-fit leads are:
- **Route businesses:** Roseville FedEx P&D, 13 routes, $2.0M / $468K CF, right at the cap. Sacramento FedEx Ground, 13 routes, $1.9M / $494K EBITDA.
- **Staffing franchises:** Spherion Woodland, $800K / $289K CF. Express Employment South Sac/Elk Grove, $250K, CF ND.
- **B2B distribution and manufacturing:** chemical distributor, $1.5M / ~$445K CF. Specialty lumber manufacturer, $995K / $294K.
- **Window & blind cleaning:** $1.4M / ~$558K CF.
- **Real-estate-backed personal-care:** Folsom salon suites with real estate, $1.8M–$1.9M / $145K CF.
- **Smaller laundromat:** Sacramento absentee laundromat, $480K / $90K CF.

Sub-$2M car washes and self-storage with business financials were essentially absent. Only real-estate (NNN or building) listings appeared.

### Inferences
- **Best absentee fits:**
  - FedEx route packages #1 (Roseville, $2.0M) and #3 (Sacramento, $1.9M). Both are about 3.8–4.3x CF; staffing for the routes needs confirming.
  - Spherion Woodland (#16), at about 2.8x CF, with a franchise support structure.
  - Chemical distributor (#24) and lumber manufacturer (#27), both about 3.4x. Manager depth is unknown.
  - Folsom salon suites with real estate (#15). The CF yield is low (~8%), but it is real-estate-backed and inherently semi-absentee.
  - Manager-run spa (#40) and semi-absentee massage franchise (#41), if financials check out.
- **Category gaps:** The Sacramento ring had **no sub-$2M car wash with business financials** and **no sub-$2M self-storage**. Car wash supply is NNN Quick Quack paper or over-cap operating washes. The Galt 3-wash portfolio (#14) may be worth asking about a single-site carve-out.
- **Laundromats:** Asking multiples run rich (5x+) versus the Regalis 3.5–4.5x benchmark.
- **Dedup signals:** Several rows may be re-lists or reprices of items in prior notes: #3 vs the BizQuest 13-route FedEx, #19 vs #18, and #37 vs the prior landscape listing. Confirm with brokers before counting them as separate opportunities.
