## 4. Local demographics / traffic

### Takeaway
Fairfield (~121K residents, median household income ~$102K) is a solid middle-income market anchored by Travis AFB, Solano County's largest employer.

### Cited Findings
- Fairfield population: 120,768 (July 1, 2023 estimate); 119,874 (April 2020 base). — [Census QuickFacts](https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fairfieldcitycalifornia/INC110223)
- Median household income $102,321 (2019-2023 ACS, 2023 dollars); poverty rate ~10.5%. — [Census QuickFacts](https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fairfieldcitycalifornia/INC110223); [Data USA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/fairfield-ca)
- Travis AFB FY2016 economic impact $1.65B; workforce 26,205 (incl. 6,641 active duty, 2,853 reservists, 1,849 civil service, 1,169 NAF/contract civilians); 4,839 indirect jobs; largest employer in Solano County. — [Travis AFB FY16 EIA](https://www.travis.af.mil/News/Article/1239638/travis-afb-releases-fy16-economic-impact-analysis/)
- Earlier: FY2013 $1.62B and 13,414 personnel. — [Travis AFB FY13 EIA](https://www.travis.af.mil/News/Display/Article/768000/travis-afb-fiscal-year-2013-economic-impact-analysis-released/)

### Inferences
- Military-household base supports discount/membership programs (Home Town already offered military discounts per 2019 article) and steady demand, but also high turnover (PCS moves) that favors convenience/express models.
