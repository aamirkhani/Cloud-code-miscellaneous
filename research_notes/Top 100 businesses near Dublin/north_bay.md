# North Bay (Solano, Napa, Marin, southern Sonoma) Businesses for Sale ≤ $2M: New Listings for an Absentee/Semi-Absentee Buyer

**Method and limits. Read first.**
- Date seen for every row: **2026-10-11**.
- Direct fetches failed with DNS errors (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`) for bizbuysell.com, bizben.com and dealstream.com. All data comes from **web-search snippets** of those pages. No listing page was opened directly.
- Search snippets do not carry posting dates. **Active status is unverified for every row** unless a note says otherwise. Older BizBen listing IDs (below ~240000) and RoutesRUs pages aged about 340 days are flagged as possibly stale.
- "CF" means the figure the portal labels "Cash Flow". That is usually SDE, but not always. Where the snippet said SDE or EBITDA, the table says so.
- "ND" means not disclosed in the snippet. Employee counts, lease terms, real estate and manager status were rarely visible.
- **Duplicate check.** I excluded everything already in `California businesses for sale under 2M/bay_area.md`:
  - Vallejo locksmith; Vacaville pool route; Fairfield Subway; Fairfield car wash; Vallejo Mission Foods route; Vacaville restaurant.
  - Napa mail centers ($150K and $200K); southern Napa sign/print franchise ($775K).
  - Marin paving, plumbing, salon/beauty school, tax practice and wellness clinic.
  - Santa Rosa dental; Santa Rosa moving & storage; Sonoma electrical contractor.
  - Solano FedEx listings: 13 vehicles at $1.4M; 9 routes in Fairfield at $1.65M; Fairfield Jimboy's.
- I also excluded the UPS Stores already covered in `UPS Store listings near Dublin/outer_ring_listings.md`: Napa at $699K, and the Marin/Sonoma bundle (Sebastopol, Petaluma, Santa Rosa, Mill Valley).
- **Buyer filters applied.** Excluded: alcohol, lottery, tobacco/vape, cannabis, gambling, pork-focused businesses, and licensed-owner medical/dental practices. Excluded items are listed separately at the end.

## Which new North Bay listings ≤ $2M fit an absentee/semi-absentee buyer?

### Takeaway
I found 30 new in-scope North Bay listings that pass the filters. 26 disclose both asking price and cash flow.

The most absentee-friendly listings are:
- **Routes:** a Fairfield 8-route FedEx at $1.175M / $345K CF, plus Mission Foods routes in Napa and Sonoma from $90K to $300K.
- **Multi-unit franchise:** a Santa Rosa 4-location tanning franchise at $1.795M, marketed as absentee, CF possibly $528,745.
- **Laundromats in Solano County:** one at $350K / $145,923 CF, and an "owner absentee" laundromat at $490K / $37,728.
- **Service businesses with staff:** a Sonoma cleaning company ("management will stay", $225K), a San Rafael staffing agency ($215K / $96K), and a Marin home-care franchise ($375K / $95K).

Solano, Napa and Marin have thin inventory in janitorial, HVAC, pest control, IT/MSP and self-storage. I found no qualifying listing in those categories.

### Inferences
- **Best fits for an absentee buyer:**
  - Fairfield 8-route FedEx (#1, 3.4x with all three figures disclosed).
  - Santa Rosa 4-location tanning franchise (#6, stated absentee). Confirm the CF.
  - Solano $350K laundromat (#7, 2.4x, lease to 2031). It is owner-operated today, but laundromats convert to absentee easily.
  - Napa Mission Foods distributorship (#12, 1.43x). It needs drivers to be absentee.
  - Sonoma cleaning company (#15). Management stays, but it has no financials and only a 2024 start.
  - San Rafael staffing (#16) and the Marin home-care franchise (#17) are semi-absentee candidates.
- Single Mission Foods routes (#10, #11, #13, #14) trade at about 1.1–1.5x CF because the CF includes the owner's driving labor. With a hired driver (roughly $50–70K/yr, an assumption, not sourced), the real multiple is about 3–4x.
- The two Solano laundromats with weak cash flow (#8 at 13x, #9 at 58x) are priced on equipment and lease value, not earnings. They are poor value unless the CF figures are wrong.
- Several snippet numbers are suspect and need a broker call before use: #4, #25, #30, and the $602K Fairfield FedEx.

### Gaps
- No listing page could be opened (DNS failures for BizBuySell, BizBen and DealStream). Listing IDs, posting dates, employee counts, rent and real-estate inclusion are mostly ND.
- No qualifying North Bay listings were found for HVAC/plumbing/electrical with managers, pest control, self-storage under $2M, IT/MSP, vending/ATM, linen or water routes, Amazon DSP, tutoring, or e-commerce.
- I found no listings at all in American Canyon or Suisun City (except over-cap real estate) and none with financials in Benicia or Dixon.
- I did not search The Bay Advisors, Sunbelt, Transworld, VR, Murphy, Franchise Clearly or LoopNet individually because of the tool-call budget. A follow-up there may surface manager-run franchise resales.
