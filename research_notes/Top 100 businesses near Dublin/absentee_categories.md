Every figure below comes from **search-engine snippets** of the cited URLs. Snippets often mix figures from adjacent listings on category pages, so every number is "as seen in snippet" and needs broker confirmation. "ND" means not disclosed in the snippets. Status is "presumed active" unless a snippet said Sold, In Contract, Inactive or Expired, or the listing's age or low ID suggests it is stale. I checked for duplicates against `California businesses for sale under 2M/bay_area.md` (its FedEx rows 38/40/43/45/46, Fairfield car wash and Mission route are **not repeated** here) and against the `UPS Store listings near Dublin/` notes (the Newark UPS Store and the SF mailbox store #291245 are **not repeated**).

## Which absentee, semi-absentee or manager-in-place listings exist in the target categories and geography?

### Takeaway
I logged 34 candidate rows. About **20 are usable leads**: in scope, ≤ $2M, with explicit absentee or manager wording and no excluded activity. About 10 have both an asking price and cash flow. Laundromats dominate the explicitly absentee inventory: Oakland, Martinez, Santa Clara County, Carmichael and Sacramento all have priced listings with cash flow. The best non-laundry fits are a semi-absentee gutter franchise ($400K ask / $162,890 CF), an Alameda County non-medical home care franchise ($888,888 / $318,035 CF), and absentee vending routes. I found **no explicitly absentee car wash under $2M** in the area; the only absentee one is a Sacramento wash at $2.46M. I found no clearly semi-absentee self-storage, Great Clips, Mathnasium/Kumon, UPS Store or fitness-franchise listing in the geography.

### Inferences
- **Best-documented in-scope leads (price + cash flow + explicit absentee wording):** #1 Oakland ($449.9K / $83.4K SDE, ~5.4x), #2 Martinez ($671.6K / $116.9K, ~5.7x), #4 Santa Clara Co. ($220K / $94.1K SDE claim, ~2.3x, but rent is 41% of revenue), #14 Carmichael ($791.5K / ~$131.9K, ~6.0x), #15 Sacramento ($350K / $70.3K, ~5.0x), and #29 gutter franchise ($400K / $162.9K, ~2.5x). #30 home care ($888.9K / $318K, ~2.8x) qualifies on numbers, but its absentee wording is unconfirmed.
- Bay Area absentee laundromats price at about **5–6x stated cash flow**. That is well above the ~2.5–3x for semi-absentee service franchises, consistent with laundromats' lower labor needs and equipment-heavy value. (I computed these multiples from the snippet figures.)
- Under-$2M absentee car washes appear essentially unavailable in the radius. Absentee car washes come with real estate and price above $2M.
- Most FedEx routes in the radius are already in `bay_area.md` or sold. Bay Advisors' current Solano listing ZBB2305 ($1.4M / $412,199 CF; "dedicated BC manager and a team of 12") is the same business as bay_area.md row 43, so I did not repeat it.
