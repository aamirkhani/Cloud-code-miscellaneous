# Alameda & Contra Costa County Businesses for Sale (≤ $2M), absentee/semi-absentee focus: new listings

**Method and limits (read first).** Research date: 2026-10-11. This adds to `research_notes/California businesses for sale under 2M/bay_area.md`. I checked rows against that file and dropped duplicates, for example the Hayward/Fremont FedEx at $925K/$243K EBITDA, the Hayward 8-route FedEx at $1.1M, the Contra Costa FedEx at $1.8M, and the Alameda IT/MSP.
- **Direct access was blocked.** WebFetch returned `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` for bizbuysell.com and bizben.com. A curl test through the agent proxy returned `CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403` for bizben.com and thebayadvisors.com. **Every figure below comes from web-search snippets** retrieved 2026-10-11. No listing page was opened.
- **Status is unverified for every row** unless a snippet stated it (sold, in escrow, pending, off market, or a posting date). Many BizBen snippets carry 2023–2025 posting dates, and I flag those as stale.
- The search tool summarizes pages and can mix up figures between adjacent listings. Suspected cases are flagged.
- "CF" means the site's "Cash Flow" field, which is usually SDE. SDE or EBITDA is named only where the snippet named it. ND = not disclosed in the snippet. Employees and real-estate inclusion were almost never visible, so they are ND unless stated.
- Buyer filters applied: no alcohol, lottery, tobacco, cannabis, gambling, pork, or licensed-owner medical/dental. Food rows in Tier C did not mention a liquor license in the snippet, but the snippets also did not confirm there is none. Verify before pursuing.

## Which new Alameda/Contra Costa listings ≤ $2M fit an absentee/semi-absentee buyer?

### Takeaway
I found 38 new in-scope listings: 33 in the table and 5 more with incomplete data. About 22 disclose both asking price and cash flow. The strongest absentee-fit candidates with full numbers are:

- Contra Costa FedEx (Bay Advisors): $1.125M / $438K CF.

- Contra Costa absentee cabinet-finishing manufacturer: $1.15M / $242K CF.
- Alameda County landscaping company: $700K / $220K CF.

Laundromat inventory is real but mostly stale (2023–2025 postings). No self-storage, car wash with business-only pricing, janitorial, pest control, or Amazon DSP listings in these two counties surfaced with financials.

- FedEx routes and the UPS Store are the only listings that explicitly or typically support a manager-in-place structure at a $1M+ cash outlay. Laundromats are the cleanest absentee option below $700K.

## What red flags are visible?

### Takeaway
The main issues are internal inconsistencies in the snippets, stale BizBen postings still indexed, cash-flow-to-revenue ratios that are implausibly high, and an unconfirmed liquor-license status on the "absentee" food listings.
