# South Bay / Peninsula / SF Businesses for Sale (Asking ≤ $2M), Absentee-Friendly, New vs. bay_area.md

## Which new listings in Santa Clara County, San Mateo County and San Francisco fit an absentee/semi-absentee buyer at ≤ $2M?

### Takeaway
I found 33 candidate rows not in `bay_area.md`, plus about 10 more I excluded as filter conflicts, stale or over the cap. Santa Clara County is the strongest pool: FedEx Ground route packages, a Mission Foods route, commercial cleaning, plumbing, education and childcare in Milpitas, absentee laundromats, vending, and a printing shop. San Mateo County adds franchise resales: Express Employment in South San Francisco, a semi-absentee math-enrichment center, Maid Brigade, senior care, and a boba franchise. San Francisco had very few qualifying absentee listings. **No status could be verified as active on 2026-10-11, and listing IDs and broker names were mostly not visible.**

### Method and limits (read first)
- **Direct fetches failed.** WebFetch to bizbuysell.com, bizben.com and bizquest.com all returned `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` on 2026-10-11, so the proxy blocks DNS for these sites. **Every figure below comes from web-search snippets or summaries retrieved 2026-10-11.**
- The search tool summarizes index pages and sometimes mixes up neighboring listings. Where figures conflict or the tool hedged, I say so.
- **"CF"** is the marketplace "Cash Flow" field, which is usually SDE but not guaranteed. EBITDA and SDE are labeled only where the snippet said so.
- **"ND"** means not disclosed in the snippet. Employees, broker names, and lease or real-estate terms were almost never visible.
- **"Seen"** is the date I retrieved each row: 2026-10-11 for all rows. The listing date is given only where the snippet showed one.
- **Status values:**
  - "Unverified" means no active/inactive flag was visible.
  - "Inactive" means the source page said "Off The Market".
  - "Stale?" means the snippet's own date is earlier than 2026.
- Multiple = asking price ÷ CF.

### Inferences
- **Best absentee-fit candidates on disclosed numbers:**
  - Express Employment, SSF: $425K / $319K CF, 1.33x.
  - San Jose 13 FedEx routes: $1.275M total / $581K CF, 2.2x.
  - Santa Clara County residential plumbing: $697K / $423K, 1.65x. It needs a manager or a licensed RMO-equivalent.
  - South Bay auto service franchise with a manager in place: $145K / $105K, 1.38x.
  - Absentee boba shops: $400K / $256K in Santa Clara County, and $350K / $180K in San Mateo.
  - Santa Clara County commercial cleaning: $700K / $200K, 3.5x.
  - Milpitas multi-location tutoring: ~$450K / $222K EBITDA.
- The San Jose FedEx row #1 (~1.9-2.2x) is priced well below Bay Area FedEx comps in bay_area.md, which run 3.2-4.2x. That gap suggests either a data error or a fleet or contract issue to diligence.
- Laundromats in this region show weak cash flow relative to price, such as the $160K store with $31.7K CF (5x). Stated CF on laundromats is often after the owner's minimal time, so the multiples are realistic but unattractive.
- San Francisco proper yielded almost no absentee-category listings under $2M beyond catering. The SF pool is restaurant-heavy and mostly fails the alcohol filter.

### Gaps
- **Active status:** none of the 37 rows could be confirmed active on 2026-10-11, because listing sites returned DNS failure (ENOTFOUND) through the proxy. Only BizBen rows #1, #8, #19, #20 and DealStream #4 have known listing IDs. The others need a manual check of the index pages.
- **Missing fields:** employees, broker names, lease terms and real-estate inclusion were almost entirely missing from the snippets. Management status is known only where the ad title said absentee, semi-absentee or manager.
- **Alcohol status:** I could not verify whether rows #18, #19, #35 and #37 or the BBQ chicken franchise sell alcohol.
- **Uncovered categories:** I found no Santa Clara, San Mateo or SF listings for pest control, landscaping, electrical, self-storage, ATM routes, IT/MSP, or UPS Store/pack-and-ship resales. Sunbelt, Transworld, VR and Murphy listings did not surface in search.
