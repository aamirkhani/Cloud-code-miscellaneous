# Additional Businesses for Sale ≤ $2M: San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Santa Cruz, San Benito and Sacramento Counties (within ~1.3 hr of Dublin, CA), Screened for an Absentee or Semi-Absentee Buyer

**Method and caveats (read first):** Research done 2026-10-11. Direct fetches failed: WebFetch to bizbuysell.com and bizben.com returned `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` (DNS blocked by the proxy). **Every figure below comes from web-search snippets** of marketplace results pages or listing pages. Consequences:
- "Date seen" is 2026-10-11 for every row. Original listing dates were almost never visible. A few snippets carried an index age (for example "~53 days" or "~190 days"), and I note those where they appeared.
- "Cash flow" (CF) is the marketplace's "cash flow" field, which is usually seller-reported SDE. I write SDE only where the snippet said so, EBITDA where it said so, and "CF (type ND)" otherwise.
- Snippets sometimes mix numbers from neighbouring listings on the same results page. I flag the cases where I saw that happen.
- **Dedup:** I checked every row against `California businesses for sale under 2M/norcal_central.md`. These were already in that file and are **not repeated** here: Watsonville ABC-47 restaurant, soil testing lab, San Joaquin snack route, Stockton pool route, Stockton trucking, Charlie's Rim Repair, the Modesto $120K franchise, and the San Joaquin $450K gas station / "auto shop" (same CF $171,812).
- Buyer filters were applied strictly. Exclusions are listed at the end.
- Abbreviations: Mgr = manager; RE = real estate; ND = not disclosed / not visible.

## What new absentee-friendly listings ≤ $2M exist in these counties?

### Takeaway
I found 38 new listings that pass the filters (30 core plus 8 lower-priority). About 17 disclose both an asking price and cash flow. The strongest absentee-type candidates are:
- Stockton 4-route FedEx linehaul ($1.275M, CF $422,844, full-time manager in place)
- Sacramento partially absentee laundromat ($350K–$400K, CF ~$70K)
- Modesto laundromats ($650K with ~$108K net; $1.7M with ~$268K CF)
- Sacramento County HVAC maintenance books ($250K/CF $124K; $500K/CF $184K)
- Santa Cruz County ServiceMaster franchise group ($1.2M, CF ~$245K)
- Santa Cruz OEM wiring-harness manufacturer ($1.1M, CF $365,697)

Many listings in Hollister, Elk Grove and Watsonville are small and owner-operated, and some had stale indexes.

### Inferences
- Implied multiples where price and CF are both shown:
  - Stockton FedEx linehaul: 3.0x
  - Sacramento laundromat: 5.0x at $350K, 5.7x at $400K (expensive versus Regalis's 3.5–4.5x range)
  - Modesto $650K laundromat: ~6.0x on seller "net"
  - Modesto $1.7M laundromat: 6.3x
  - Modesto juice bar: 4.95x
  - Sac HVAC: 2.0x and 2.7x
  - Santa Cruz ServiceMaster: 4.9x
  - Wiring harness: 3.0x
  - Stockton distribution: 1.7x
  - Watsonville firewood with RE: 3.4x
  - Hollister pool route: 0.87x
- On these numbers the laundromats here are priced rich. The HVAC books and the Stockton distribution business screen cheapest, but they are probably owner-dependent.
- The Stockton FedEx linehaul is the only listing that explicitly states a full-time manager is in place. Linehaul is generally more absentee-friendly than Ground P&D, but it carries contract risk with FedEx and vehicle capex.
- Several Santa Cruz figures look cross-contaminated:
  - The "$1,170,000 / $237,627" pair is attributed to both a soil lab and a nationwide DumpStor franchise.
  - The "$2.5M / $337,353" pair is attributed to both a Watsonville wood manufacturer and (in the earlier file) a Santa Cruz restaurant.
  - "$78,500 / $27,000" is attributed to both a Watsonville auto parts business and a residential cleaning business.
  
  For that reason I left out the duplicate-number items: auto parts, residential cleaning, DumpStor, and the $2.5M wood manufacturer.
