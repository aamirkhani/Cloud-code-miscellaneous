# Mapping 190 California Businesses Under $2M

**About 190 California businesses with asking prices of $2M or less turned up in marketplace search results on October 9, 2026.** They span the Bay Area, Los Angeles and Orange County, San Diego and the Inland Empire, and Sacramento, the Central Valley, the Central Coast and the far north. Restaurants, auto repair, trades, FedEx routes, dental and medical practices, and franchise resales make up most of them. Most ask **1.5x to 3.5x seller-reported cash flow**, which is in line with the national closed-deal average of **2.7x SDE** and a California median asking price of about **$360,000** ([BizBuySell CA](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california-businesses-for-sale/4/); [Small Business Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/small-business-acquisitions-drop-10-as-buyers-get-more-selective/)). On reported numbers, the best values are S&S Welding in San Jose (0.93x, $324K cash flow), the 10-route FedEx Ground package in Redding (1.62x on $448K), the Fallbrook six-bay auto shop (1.25x), the LA ramen franchise and Santa Ana sushi restaurant (about 1.3x to 1.4x with revenue disclosed), and the San Bernardino logistics company (1.38x, owner-financed). **One caveat applies to every figure here.** The research network blocked direct access to every listing marketplace, so all prices, revenues and cash flows come from search-engine extracts of category and county results pages. They do not come from opened listing pages. No listing's active status, posting date or real-estate inclusion could be confirmed, some extracts pair a price with the wrong listing, and several rows were already marked sold or pending. Treat each row as a lead to confirm with the listing broker, not as a verified offer.

## Every number here is an unverified search extract

The four regional research passes all hit the same wall. BizBuySell, BizQuest, BizBen, BusinessBroker.net, DealStream, BizTrader, Sunbelt, Rejigg and others returned "egress blocked" from the network proxy. As a result, each "source" link below points to the **marketplace category or county page** where the listing appeared in search results, not to the listing itself. To find a listing, open that page and search for the business type and price. The consequences are concrete. Listing dates were invisible. Some Sunbelt and BizBen pages were flagged as 1,600 or more days old. Two listings were marked sale pending and one off market, and the search tool warned that it sometimes mixed up figures from neighboring listings on a results page.

"Cash flow" is the marketplace's generic field. On BizBuySell and BizQuest it normally means **seller's discretionary earnings (SDE)**, which includes one owner's salary and perks. A few listings labeled the figure EBITDA or "net income", and the tables note those. The multiple is asking price divided by cash flow, calculated by the researchers. "ND" means not disclosed in the extract. Real-estate inclusion and year established were mostly invisible, so the tables mention them only where an extract stated them.

Low multiples deserve skepticism. Most sub-1.5x listings are owner-operated restaurants, auto shops and trades, so their SDE includes the owner's full-time wage. A buyer who hires a manager pays a much higher effective multiple. Sub-1x listings, such as the Modesto rim repair (0.87x) and the SLO mobile auto interior business (0.88x), amount to buying a job. FedEx route multiples also price in contract-renewal risk, vehicle capex and the need for FedEx to approve the buyer. The Stockton gas station with land, a house and a beer/wine license at **$450K (2.62x including real estate)** is the most anomalous "bargain" here. It needs a Phase I environmental review for underground tanks before anyone treats it as value ([BizBuySell San Joaquin Co.](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-joaquin-county-businesses-for-sale/)).

## Red flags cluster in five patterns

**Implausible cash flow is the most dangerous flag, because it makes a listing look like the best deal on the page.** The Vacaville pool route claims $468K cash flow on a $125K ask (0.27x), which is almost certainly a data error ([BizBuySell Solano](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/solano-county-businesses-for-sale/)). The San Diego two-location cafe bakery shows **$992K cash flow on ~$1.32M revenue, a 75% margin** that no food business earns ([BizBuySell San Diego](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-diego-businesses-for-sale/)). The Koreatown restaurant shows $480K "net income" on a $300K ask, the SF dental practice's cash flow exceeds its price, and the Sacramento pool fence company and North County SD health practice claim margins of about 70% and 67% ([BizBuySell LA](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/los-angeles-businesses-for-sale/); [BizBuySell Bay Area health care](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/san-francisco-bay-area/health-care-and-fitness-businesses-for-sale/); [BizBuySell Sacramento](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/sacramento-established-businesses-for-sale/2/)).

**Overpricing is the second pattern.** The worst case is a Los Angeles restaurant asking **$1.35M on just $23,750 cash flow (56.8x)**, which suggests the price reflects lease, location or equipment rather than earnings ([BizBuySell LA](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/los-angeles-businesses-for-sale/)). Small manufacturers ask the most relative to earnings. The LA pressure-washer/trailer maker is at 7.83x, the San Fernando Valley fiber-optic stretcher maker at 6.56x and the Chico textile equipment maker at 6.33x, all roughly double the 3.0x manufacturing average ([BizBuySell manufacturing benchmarks](https://www.bizbuysell.com/learning-center/valuation-benchmarks/manufacturing/)). The SF grocery market (6.61x EBITDA), the Tahoe City laundromat (4.81x) and The UPS Store in San Diego (4.04x, plus franchise fees) also sit well above their segment norms.

**Missing figures are the third pattern.** Every LA dental listing (Glendale, Lawndale, Palms, Norwalk and Central LA) and the $900K LA home-care business hide cash flow ([BizBuySell LA Co. dental](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/los-angeles-county/dental-practices-for-sale/)). Several restaurants show high revenue but no cash flow: Morro Bay ($175K on $1.23M), Monterey ($900K on $4.29M), Walnut Creek ($395K on $2.1M) and Chico ($350K on $1.34M). Restaurants usually sell at about **0.41x revenue**, so these probably have thin margins or lease problems ([BizBuySell industry multiples](https://www.bizbuysell.com/learning-center/industry-valuation-multiples/)). Thin margins show up openly elsewhere. The San Mateo freight forwarder earns 4.2% on $1.95M, the Santa Rosa moving company earns 8.4% at a 4.0x ask, and the Bakersfield Mrs. Fields earns $15,557 on $223,144, which is barely a wage.

**Hidden consideration is the fourth pattern.** Some headline prices understate the total cost. Several prices are quoted "plus inventory" (Fresno postal store, Fresno restaurant, Monterey liquor store). The Redding FedEx package adds ~$275K of vehicle financing. A Fairfield FedEx package lists $850K cash required against a **$1.7M total purchase price** ([BizBuySell Solano](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/solano-county-businesses-for-sale/)). Real estate inflates prices just over the cap. One European auto shop asks $575K for the business plus $1.6M for property, and a Riverside County collision shop with real estate asks $3.2M on $97K cash flow ([BizBuySell Riverside County](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/riverside-county-businesses-for-sale/)).

**Stale and conflicting data is the fifth pattern.** Sunbelt LA pages, including those for the jewelry distributor, candy maker and lock distributor, were indexed at roughly 1,600 to 1,700 days old. Several Bay Area FedEx routes in the results had already sold, and the Sonoma electrical contractor appears at two prices on two sites ([Sunbelt LA County](https://www.sunbeltnetwork.com/business-search/business-results/county-los-angeles-state-california/); [The Bay Advisors](https://thebayadvisors.com/profitable-fedex-routes-for-sale-san-francisco-bay-area/); [BusinessBroker.net Sonoma](https://www.businessbroker.net/california/sonoma-county-businesses-for-sale.aspx)). One gap applies everywhere: **no listing showed multi-year revenue**, so the research could not detect declining revenue for any listing.

## Benchmarks: $350K deals at 2.7x SDE, with California-specific frictions

The national closed-deal market is stable. In Q2 2026, **2,117 businesses sold at a median price of $349,250, median revenue of $692,087 and median cash flow of $155,921**, at an average **2.7x cash flow and 0.7x revenue**. Deal volume fell 10% year over year ([Small Business Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/small-business-acquisitions-drop-10-as-buyers-get-more-selective/)). In 2025, businesses sold for about **94% of asking** at an average 2.61x ([BizBuySell 2025 Year in Review](https://www.bizbuysell.com/blog/2025-year-in-review/)). BizBuySell's LA County restaurant page puts the typical sale at 80% to 90% of asking ([BizBuySell LA Co. restaurants](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california/los-angeles-county/restaurants-and-food-businesses-for-sale/)). California has about **5,500 listings on BizBuySell with a median asking price of about $360,000** ([BizBuySell CA](https://www.bizbuysell.com/california-businesses-for-sale/4/)). Multiples rise with deal size, from about 2.0x SDE under $500K to about 2.5x to 2.8x at $500K to $1M and about 3.0x to 3.3x at $1M to $2M. The two published summaries of the IBBA data disagree slightly ([FISART](https://fisart.com/en/blog/ebitda-multiples); [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/26/business-valuation-multiples-by-industry-sde-ebitda-guide)). Across more than 27,500 US listings, the median *asking* multiple is 2.63x SDE, with an interquartile range of 1.73x to 3.72x ([BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/how-to-value-a-small-business)).

Financing sets a practical ceiling on what these listings can command. SBA 7(a) loans go up to $5M, with variable rates capped near **Prime + 3% (about 9.75% in April 2026)** for loans over $350K ([sba7a.loans](https://www.sba7a.loans/sba-7a-loans-small-business-blog/what-is-the-sba-7a-loan-interest-rate/)). SOP 50 10 8, effective June 2025, restored a **10% minimum equity injection**, and seller notes now count toward it only if they stay on full standby for the life of the loan ([PilieroMazza](https://pilieromazza.com/five-sba-7a-changes-that-could-reshape-business-acquisitions)). A further tightening, SOP 50 10 8.1, was reportedly effective October 1, 2026, but only one secondary source reports it ([Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/23/sba-sop-50-10-8-1-change-of-ownership-equity-dscr-qoe-guide)). Lenders underwrite on tax returns and ignore claimed unreported cash ([Arkansas SBTDC](https://asbtdc.org/buying-a-business-6-things-to-consider/)). Any listing whose multiple only works on undocumented SDE will not finance.

California adds its own costs. The state minimum wage is **$16.90 in 2026, and $20 for chain fast food**, so franchise SDE from before April 2024 overstates current earnings ([CalChamber](https://hrwatchdog.calchamber.com/?p=17820); [Paycor](https://www.paycor.com/resource-center/articles/california-minimum-wage/)). Buyers must hold back purchase funds in escrow for CDTFA sales-tax and EDD payroll-tax clearances. In *Schnyder v. State Board of Equalization*, a buyer who escrowed funds but skipped the clearance was still held liable ([CDTFA](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol1/sutcd/sales-and-use-tax-court-decisions-s5.html); [Stimmel Law](https://stimmel-law.com/articles/bulk-sales-law-sale-assets-california/)). Liquor-license deals such as the Oakland and Watsonville Type 47 restaurants require the full price to sit in escrow until ABC approves the transfer ([B&P §24074](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_24074)).

## Conclusion

The search-index snapshot shows a California market where a buyer with $200K to $750K has dozens of options at or below the 2.7x national norm. Above $1.25M the choice narrows to FedEx routes, contractors, franchise portfolios and a few manufacturers, and that band is effectively empty outside the Bay Area and Southern California. The listings that look cheapest are disproportionately the least reliable. Of the eight listings below 1.0x, seven carry a suspect-data or owner-labor flag. The most actionable shortlist is therefore the mid-1x to 2x listings with full disclosure: S&S Welding, the LA ramen franchise, the Chico bagel café, the San Bernardino logistics company, the Redding FedEx package, the Santa Rosa dental practice and the San Diego plumbing contractor.

The next step is mechanical. Open each shortlisted listing on its marketplace and confirm with the broker that it is still active and that the price, revenue and cash flow match the extracts. Then ask for three years of tax returns, an add-back schedule, owner hours and lease terms. Re-cast labor at 2026 California wage rates before comparing anything to the multiples in this report. Many of these leads will fall away at that step. Even so, an unverified snapshot of about 190 listings tells a buyer which 10 to 15 brokers to call first.
