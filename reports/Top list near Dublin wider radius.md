## The method is unchanged except for the 95-minute radius

The script `research_notes/Top list near Dublin wider radius/wider_rank_calc.py` is a copy of the previous `rerank_calc.py`. It keeps the same columns and the same formulas: adjusted cash flow, adjusted multiple, all-cash yield, a 13% out-of-pocket estimate, a 90% SBA 7(a) loan at the maximum variable rate, debt-service coverage, and the score. The score is the adjusted multiple times a risk weight (Low 1.0 to High 2.0), times 0.85 when the listing states absentee or manager-in-place [A], times 1.15 when the listing is stale or its figures conflict [S]. A row must clear **$40K of adjusted cash flow** to rank. Every table below is the script's unedited output. The script also recomputes the old ranking from the previous file, so the "Prev #" column shows where each row stood in the 80-minute list.

The script makes four changes to the inputs. First, the radius check moves from 80 to 95 minutes. Second, rows excluded earlier only for distance come back in. The only one with both a price and a cash flow is the **Carmichael absentee-run laundry ($791.5K, $131.9K, lease to 2040)** ([BizBen Sacramento](https://www.bizben.com/business-for-sale/laundromat-for-sale-sacramento-california-192539.php)). The Sebastopol UPS Store, the Roseville Golden Rose LTC pharmacy and the stale Salinas UPS Store also re-entered the radius, but they have no cash flow, so they go to the partial-data appendix. Third, the 46 Sacramento-ring rows and 35 Monterey/Merced/Sonoma-ring rows from this folder's two notes files were deduplicated against all earlier notes. Each was ranked, or sent to the matching appendix for pizza or pork, alcohol, cannabis, licensed-owner professions, prices over $2M, sold listings or missing data. Fourth, where the new notes disagree with earlier ones, the new notes win. That is why BizBen 2810644 falls below the floor. It is also why the old "South Bay printing shop" is now identified as the Gilroy full-service printer: its $495K/$191K figures match BizBen 292848, posted in May 2025 and relisted since, so it is now marked [S] and falls from #39 to #70 ([BizBen 292848](https://www.bizben.com/business-for-sale/printing-shop-for-sale-in-south-bay-area-california-292848.php)).
