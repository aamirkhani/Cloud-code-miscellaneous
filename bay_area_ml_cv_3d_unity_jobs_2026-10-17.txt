HOW TO READ THIS FILE
---------------------
* Counts are ESTIMATES of currently open roles in the six target fields that are
  located in the SF Bay Area (SF, Peninsula, South Bay, East Bay) or open to
  remote-US candidates. They are NOT exact counts pulled from each careers site.
* Direct access to company careers sites / ATS APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby,
  Workday) was blocked in the research environment, so numbers were built from
  web-search snapshots of job aggregators (LinkedIn, TrueUp, Built In, Autobound,
  FastAIJobs, Metaintro, Hitmarker, VC job boards, Remotive) from ~Jun-Oct 2026.
* Treat every number as +/- 50% or worse. Rank order is more reliable than the
  absolute numbers. Postings open and close daily - always verify on the careers URL.
* Sorted by the MIDPOINT of the estimated range (highest first).

CONFIDENCE
  [S] = backed by web-search evidence of actual 2026 postings / aggregator counts
  [K] = not live-checked; estimate based on the company's known team size and
        typical hiring pattern (lower confidence - verify first)

FIELD TAGS
  ML = machine learning   DL = deep learning   CV = computer vision / perception
  3D = 3D vision/geometry GFX = 3D graphics / rendering / GPU
  REC = 3D reconstruction (SLAM, SfM, NeRF, Gaussian splatting)
  UNITY = Unity / game-engine development

REMOTE column = how often relevant roles are open to remote-US candidates
  (Yes / Many / Some / Rare / No)
